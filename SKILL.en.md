# E-commerce Visual Planning and Execution

> Full English companion to the canonical runtime entrypoint, `SKILL.md`. English and bilingual output must preserve the same evidence, compliance, routing, and execution rules.

Turn raw product materials into a visual plan a designer or image model can execute. Default to Chinese; switch language when requested without changing factual scope.

## Core principles

1. **Check evidence and legality separately**: start with user-supplied product, packaging, specification and qualification materials for facts. Applicable mandatory law cannot be overridden by those materials or a user request. Evidence does not by itself make an advertising claim lawful; assumptions cannot fill factual gaps.
2. **Route by intent**: deliver the requested layer, without forcing a full workflow or asking for information already supplied.
3. **Never turn assumptions into facts**: do not invent qualifications, tests, patents, sales, reviews, rankings, efficacy, certifications, or platform rules.
4. **Lock visual identity before execution**: product identity, composition, palette, lighting, typography, background, reference fidelity, and negative constraints must stay consistent.
5. **Treat platform rules as time-sensitive**: verify official sources when current dimensions, image restrictions, category qualifications or claim policies matter. Label unverified requirements.
6. **Do not provide final legal conclusions** for food, supplements, medical devices, health care, children's goods or other high-risk categories. Check the target market and category.

The skill does not cover pure short-video/live-selling scripts, advertising or store-operations optimization, or final lawyer-level legal conclusions (risk screening is supported), and does not automatically publish listings.

## Execution modes

**Shared output contract**: read `references/output-contracts.md` before a complete plan or formal audit in any mode, including a full Standard strategy/storyboard. Small isolated wording edits do not require the entire template.

### Standard

Use when critical evidence or direction is missing, or the user asks for strategy/storyboard before execution.

1. Deliver the visual strategy, evidence ledger and evidence gaps together.
2. Deliver the supported storyboard; mark unresolved parts rather than inventing scene details.
3. **Pause once, after the Storyboard**, combining necessary evidence requests and direction review. After confirmation, proceed to execution and quality gates without per-image approvals.

### One-shot

If the user asks for a complete answer in one pass, or inputs are sufficient, complete strategy, storyboard, execution cards, and quality gates without unnecessary pauses.

### Audit / revision

For existing images, copy, prompts, or PDPs, identify problems in conversion logic, hierarchy, evidence/compliance, platform fit, image/copy alignment, and style drift. Make the minimum necessary changes.

### Designer handoff

Use the shared output contract for cards that a designer or image model can execute without guessing.

### Default mode (when the user does not specify)

Explicit mode, sequence and checkpoint requests take priority. Otherwise, first match wins:

1. Existing finished images, PDPs, copy or prompts to review/revise → **Audit / revision**. Source product/packaging photos alone do not trigger Audit.
2. A brief for a designer or operator to execute directly → **Designer handoff**.
3. Product, platform/market, audience, necessary assets and evidence suffice for this scope → **One-shot**. Do not require three selling points just to fill a quota.
4. Missing critical evidence, unclear platform/audience or direction → **Standard**, with one combined checkpoint after the Storyboard.

State the selected mode in one sentence. For explicitly requested One-shot or handoff with missing materials, complete supported parts and list blockers outside publishable copy without fabricating facts or implying unsupported work is complete.

## Step 0 — Task routing and minimum inputs

Identify the requested surface: main images, PDP/detail page, Amazon Listing/A+/Brand Story, ad images/video covers, on-image copy, storyboard, image prompts, compliance review/revision, or cross-border localization.

Prioritize product identity, platform/market, audience, selling points/specifications, evidence, and source/reference images. Request at most three critical missing inputs only when they materially change positioning, compliance, or key scenes; in Standard mode, combine these at the single post-Storyboard checkpoint. Otherwise continue with explicit presentation assumptions, never invented product facts.

## Step 1 — Build an evidence ledger

Every factual, numerical, efficacy or qualification claim intended for the final page enters the ledger first.

| Claim | Source | Status | Final treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Directly supported by materials whose product/scope has been checked | Packaging image, specification sheet, specific document | Verified | Preserve meaning; a verbal assertion does not establish testing/certification |
| Benefit derived from a verified parameter | Same source | Supported | Translate value without expanding scope |
| Claim requiring a test, certification, rule or approval | Missing evidence | Needs proof | Use neutral wording or keep in internal pending items |
| Fabricated, misleading or unsupported regulated claim | No lawful support | Disallowed | Exclude from final copy |

Only Verified and Supported claims may appear as facts. Record a file/page, image ID or official URL, applicable SKU, market, conditions and date when relevant. An example image establishes its appearance, not the truth of its origin, certification, specifications or commercial claims. Placeholders belong in internal notes, never publishable copy.

If a claim is verbal and the original material has not been inspected, record that source honestly rather than saying the material was verified. A structure or material alone does not establish performance: "has feet/pads" cannot become "stable placement" or "non-slip" without supporting evidence. Calling this a "neutral scene description" does not remove the evidence requirement.

## Step 2 — Select the conversion driver

Choose one primary and at most one secondary driver.

| Driver | Typical use | Visual emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Visual / sensory | Beauty, jewelry, home, gift boxes | Materials, light, proportion, packaging |
| Pain point / efficiency | Cleaning, storage, tools | Use context, process, fewer steps |
| Rational / parameter-led | Electronics, appliances, equipment | Specifications, mechanisms, compatibility |
| Trust / evidence-led | Food, tea, baby products, high-value goods | Origin, craft, qualifications, traceable evidence |
| Emotional / identity-led | Fragrance, apparel, gifting, pets | Aesthetic, rituals, use scenes, identity |
| Price / bundle / promotion | Pinduoduo, Temu, Douyin, kits/refills | Included items, unit/bundle price, conditions, dates |

Translate up to three supported purchase reasons for the requested scope with `Feature -> Advantage -> Benefit -> Evidence`; never invent extra reasons to fill three slots. Conversion pressure never justifies unsupported claims.

For price-led work verify currency, SKU, quantity, unit/bundle total, eligibility, thresholds, start/end dates, and shipping. If evidence is missing, show known bundle contents only; do not invent reference prices, discounts, lowest-price claims, inventory, timers, or gifts.

## Step 3 — Lock the Campaign Style

Define:

- **Product identity**: immutable packaging text, logo, color, proportions, structure, materials and details.
- **Composition**: product coverage, camera angle, whitespace, reading direction, people/props.
- **Palette**: primary, supporting and accent colors.
- **Lighting**: natural/studio, hard/soft light and rim lighting.
- **Typography/density**: headline, numbers, body and mobile readability.
- **Background/scenarios**: plain, studio, real-world, or brand atmosphere.
- **Reference Fidelity**: what must stay and what may change.
- **Negative Constraints**: forbidden new logos, accessories, textures, anatomy, structure or efficacy symbols.

Use the real uploaded product/packaging as the source of truth. Decorative improvement must not change packaging, flavor, size, logo, accessories or structure.

## Step 4 — Plan the storyboard

Do not hard-code five images. Five is a starting point; adapt to platform, user scope and actual information.

| Role | Decision task |
|---|---|
| Hero / KV | Identify the product and one reason to click |
| Benefit | Translate a key feature into customer value |
| Proof | Establish trust with real evidence |
| Compare | Reduce selection friction using comparable facts |
| Scene | Show relevant use contexts |
| Spec / CTA | Explain variants, bundles, conditions or the next decision |

A PDP may follow: `Opening -> Top Benefits -> Mechanism/material/craft -> Scenes -> Proof -> Selection -> FAQ -> Specs/CTA`. Omit unnecessary modules.

Load the relevant section of `references/platform-playbooks.md` for platform work. Keep Douyin Shop separate from TikTok Shop, and use the separate Xiaohongshu and WeChat Channels/WeChat Shop sections. Verify current rules using `references/source-notes.md` when dimensions, text permissions, claims or category requirements matter.

## Step 5 — Execute

For each image/module deliver:

1. **Visual task**: the decision question it solves.
2. **Scene/composition**: subject, camera, background, materials, light and hierarchy.
3. **On-image copy**: one headline and necessary short support, checked against the copy specifications below.
4. **Design rationale**: first focus, reading order, emphasis and annotation/disclaimer placement.
5. **Image-generation prompt**: subject + composition + scene + materials + lighting + Style Lock + reference constraints + text rendering method.
6. **Negative Prompt / forbidden changes**: packaging distortion, incorrect logos/specs, new structure, text artifacts, anatomy errors and style drift.
7. **Evidence notes** for claims, numbers, qualifications or comparisons.

Ten-line minimal execution card. Teaching inputs: a structure drawing and adjustment instructions are supplied; 20kg capacity is only a verbal assertion without test evidence and is excluded from copy.

```text
KV2 — Benefit | Adjustable kitchen rack / Taobao (teaching example)
Task: show adjustment structure and countertop footprint
Scene: two adjustment states from the real structure drawing; preserve proportions and Style Lock
Copy: headline "Adjustable countertop storage"; support "See the mechanism before choosing a size"; no number tag
Layout: structure comparison first; upper-left whitespace and bottom annotation space
Text rendering method: Layout-reserved; designer adds typography in post-production
Copy-spec self-check: 3 headline words; one 7-word support line; zero number tags
Prompt: follow structure references, soft light, pale gray background, upper-left whitespace, no new promotional text
Negative Prompt: distorted structure/logo, extra accessories, promotional text, watermark
Evidence/platform: drawing supports adjustment; 20kg needs proof and is excluded; current platform rules unverified
```

For cross-border work, preserve factual scope while adapting units, use cases, cultural context, language length and purchase reasoning rather than translating literally.

## On-image copy and text rendering

### Copy specifications (defaults, not platform rules)

| Surface | Suggested ceiling |
|---|---|
| Main image | One headline (≤12 Chinese characters / ≤6 English words) + at most two support lines (each ≤18 characters / ≤10 words) + at most one number or evidence tag |
| Independent PDP module | One headline (≤12 characters / ≤6 words) + at most three points (each ≤18 characters / ≤12 words); number/evidence content counts toward the points |
| Disclaimers/annotations | Distinct readable bottom area; material conditions also sit beside the relevant claim |

Count numbers and punctuation, exclude whitespace for Chinese; adapt other languages to comparable visual width. Number/evidence tags are optional and must already be in the evidence ledger. Keep specification tables and FAQs separate. **Cut or split images/modules when over the limit; do not shrink type to force a fit.** Explicit layout needs may adjust these suggestions with a reason, without relaxing factual or platform constraints.

For the copy-spec self-check, count each final headline and individual support/point string. Count English words by whitespace. With execution tools, run `python scripts/count-copy.py --language en "Final headline" "Final support"` (omit the language flag for Chinese), or use an equivalent counter; claiming a manual recount is insufficient. Without a counter, state that lengths await tool verification instead of estimating them. Recalculate after edits.

- At a 1080px canvas width, use at least 72px for headlines, 42px for support, and 36px for necessary annotations/disclaimers; scale proportionally. This is the skill's internal readability baseline, not a platform rule. Check at about 360px display width. Shorten copy, split modules or move parameters into page text rather than shrinking type.
- Put disclaimers/evidence annotations in a distinct readable bottom area with a safe inset and sufficient contrast. Material price/efficacy conditions must also sit next to the claim. Preserve required approved statements verbatim; these are exempt from marketing word limits, so adapt the layout.

### Text rendering method (choose exactly one per card)

- **Layout-reserved (default)**: specify text-safe-zone position, proportion and background brightness; generate no new promotional text, prices, badges or watermarks. Deliver a separate final text layer for designer/layout-tool post-production. Preserve existing packaging text and logos, compositing the original product image if needed.
- **Model-rendered (exception)**: only when the user explicitly asks and the text is very short (default ≤8 Chinese characters or one English headline of ≤6 words). Quote exact strings, language, type style, position and hierarchy. For longer text, deliver Layout-reserved and explain why.
- Include **text acceptance and fallback** in every card. Inspect every character after model rendering; typos, missing text, gibberish or packaging rewrites are Fail conditions. Fall back to Layout-reserved plus post-production typography. Without an inspected image, state that text rendering is pending acceptance. If the platform disallows overlays, provide no new promotional text.

## Step 6 — Five-part quality gate

Check hard blockers first:

- Invented or untraceable qualifications, tests, certification, sales, reviews, rankings, patents or numbers.
- Product identity conflicts with source materials.
- Needs proof written as fact.
- Unverified current platform rules stated as certain requirements.
- Unsupported medical/health guarantees or claims outside approved scope.
- Mainland China advertising with prohibited claims, missing statutory ad approval, or required citation sources/conditions hidden in internal notes or unreadable text.

Five Pass/Fail dimensions:

| Dimension | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Evidence & compliance | Important claims traceable; risks removed or downgraded |
| Conversion logic | Each image has a separate decision task; top reasons clear |
| Visual hierarchy | Clear first focus; readable density and subject proportion |
| Consistency | Style Lock, product identity, type, palette and light maintained |
| Executability | No guessing about essential production details |

Fix every Fail before delivery; success elsewhere cannot compensate. Check word limits, rendering responsibilities, disclaimer placement and mobile preview. If the user explicitly wants numerical scoring, use explained 1–5 anchors, not arbitrary 80/100 precision.

## Compliance and currency

Read `references/compliance-rules.md` for compliance work and locate official sources by category in `references/source-notes.md`. Distinguish reviewed provisions from login-only policy entry points. An accessible entry point does not verify a specific rule.

### Mainland China main-image and PDP advertising rules

Before writing or revising main-image/PDP copy for mainland China on Taobao/Tmall, JD, Pinduoduo, Douyin Shop, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels/WeChat Shop or similar surfaces, **read section 10 of `references/compliance-rules.md`** and check the applicable PRC Advertising Law sources. Target market determines the rules regardless of output language.

- Establish the product category and permitted claims before converting materials into benefits. Do not copy unlawful or misleading source wording. Reference fidelity does not excuse problematic packaging claims: flag the blocker and obtain compliant assets without silently altering the product.
- Default China marketing copy excludes absolute superlatives such as “国家级、最高级、最佳”, unsupported leadership/uniqueness/lowest-price claims and medical-effect promises. Do not evade restrictions with homophones, pinyin, split characters, symbols or visual implications. For a potentially substantiated exception, provide a plain factual alternative and put the original outside final copy for human review. Human review cannot authorize a prohibited claim. Distinguish objective instructions such as “第一步” from superiority marketing; do not ban individual characters mechanically.
- Ledger the evidence for numbers, reports, rankings and comparisons, then check legal permissibility. **Required citation sources, sample/conditions, scope and validity belong in consumer-visible text**, not only the ledger. Distinguish advertiser self-tests from external citations; AI-generated data is not verified evidence.
- Prices, gifts, promotions and service promises must match confirmed terms. Keep material conditions beside the claim, not hidden in low-contrast small print or an “actual product prevails” disclaimer. Check patent/certification scope and endorsement/testimonial rights; invent no official approval.
- Ordinary goods cannot use disease-treatment claims or wording that confuses them with drugs/devices. If statutory ad approval is required but missing, provide a verification checklist and non-publishable structure only. Do not freely edit an approved advertisement; preserve applicable mandatory statements.
- Every card includes a **China advertising compliance check**, with a separate final text layer and risk/source notes. Blocked or pending-human-review claims are not publishable. Continue supported portions; image acceptance remains pending until inspected. Do not promise Advertising Law certification or platform approval.

- A disclaimer cannot repair an impermissible claim.
- Absolute, comparison, price, certification, health, efficacy and review claims depend on evidence and target market.
- Ordinary food, health food/supplements, medical devices, care products and children's goods require different claim boundaries.
- Laws, platform rules and category requirements change; prefer current official documents when available. This framework is not legal advice.

## Key failure modes

- **Over-questioning**: inputs suffice but questions continue. Use explicit presentation assumptions and proceed.
- **Mechanical checkpoints**: forcing stops despite one-shot intent. Follow the selected mode.
- **Fixed five images**: padding an image set without decision tasks. Adapt count.
- **Selling-point overload**: multiple purchase reasons per image. Use one primary task.
- **Outdated policy**: presenting historical practice as current requirements. Verify official sources.
- **Keyword-only compliance**: deleting words without checking the underlying claim. Review fact -> evidence -> consumer interpretation.
- **Literal localization**: translating without adapting purchase reasoning, units and context.
- **Reference drift**: changing packaging/structure for aesthetics. Preserve real product identity.
- **Example treated as proof**: sample artwork does not substantiate its own claims. Check original materials.
- **All text handed to the model**: long text is error-prone. Choose one method per card; default to Layout-reserved, with model rendering only for explicitly requested short text followed by character-by-character review.
- **Copy overload**: headline, support, tags and disclaimers crowd one image. Cut or split according to the copy specifications, never shrink to fit.
- **Promotion fabrication**: no evidence means no invented reference price, lowest-price claim, time limit, inventory urgency, discount or gift.
- **Evidence mistaken for legality**: copying a documented claim or changing characters without removing a prohibited medical/guarantee meaning. Check category and legal restrictions first; make citation conditions visible.
